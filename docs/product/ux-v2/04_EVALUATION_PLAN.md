# Evaluating the V2 prototype with real businesses

> **Status:** PROPOSED plan, 2026-11-02 (ticket UX-15). No sessions have run. Nothing in this folder is user-validated.

## Purpose

Find out, before large-scale frontend work, whether real haulers and landing operators can complete the two core journeys in the prototype, and whether the information they see is enough to decide.

## Participants

| Group | Count | Screen for |
|---|---|---|
| Owner-operators (one or two trucks) | 3 | Hauls logs today; mostly uses a phone |
| Hauling companies (three or more trucks) | 2 | Owner or office manager who finds work for several trucks |
| Landing operators or logging contractors | 3 | Has recruited trucks in the last six months, ideally one with several landings |
| Dual-role (optional) | 1 | Runs a landing and a hauling business |

Recruit through the founder's existing relationships. Do not use the seeded development personas, and do not store names, phone numbers or company names in the repository.

## Setup

- Use the participant's own phone where possible. Otherwise use a 390 px browser window.
- Open the prototype link from #116. Explain that it is a design sketch with made-up businesses and that nothing they enter is saved.
- About 30 minutes per session. Think-aloud. The moderator does not help unless the participant is stuck for more than 60 seconds.

## Tasks

### Haulers

1. "You've just heard about LogLoads. Set up so you can look for work for your truck(s)." (A1–A5)
2. "Find a job you might take this week." What do they look at first? Do they find pay, equipment and distance without help?
3. "Is this job worth a call? Why or why not?" (A7) Note any missing fact they ask for.
4. "Tell this landing you're interested." (A8) Time it, and count taps.
5. "The landing replied. What do they want, and what will you do?" (A9)
6. Fleets only: "How many of your trucks can you offer next week?" (A-fleet)

### Landing operators

1. "Set up your company and the landing you're working now." (B1–B3) Ask what they would put in the private panel and whether they trust it to stay private.
2. "Post the next job you need trucks for." (B4–B7) Do the required fields match how they describe jobs today? Do they understand the three attestations?
3. "You have three inquiries. Which hauler would you call first, and why?" (B9)
4. "What would make you pay $149, $299 or $499 a month for this?" Listen only; do not negotiate. The pricing is LOCKED.

## What to record

For each task: completed (yes, with help, no), time, the point of hesitation, and a direct quote. Afterwards, ask each participant:

- What was missing that you'd need before calling or replying?
- What here would you not trust? (verification, availability, pay)
- Anything you'd never fill in?

## Questions the sessions should answer

1. Is the seven-fact job summary (pay and basis, equipment, area, destination and miles, start and duration, trucks, freshness) enough to decide?
2. Do haulers understand "self-reported" and "confirmed by the poster 2 days ago"?
3. Do landing operators accept the required pay disclosure (founder decision C1)?
4. Does the public/private split for landings and profiles feel safe?
5. Is the one-tap interest with prefilled trucks and dates accurate enough for landings?
6. Do fleet owners keep a capacity board current, and how often?

## Reporting

Post a summary on UX-15 with counts per task, quotes and the proposed design changes. Mark any task with fewer than three sessions as **not yet tested**.
