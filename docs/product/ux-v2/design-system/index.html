LogLoads design system and its key screens

One system across the hauler, fleet and landing workspaces. Every screen is composed from the same layout and marketplace components and styled only by tokens.css and components.css, which are separate files published with this page. The developer check below rewrites a few semantic tokens to show that one change reaches every page. It is a review tool, not a user feature.

Simulated: sending interest or messages, confirming availability and reconfirming a job change only this page. Nothing is published, sent or saved. Row selection, density, the details panel and dialogs work as they would in the product.

What changed

  • No dark bands. Navigation, page headers and the company header are white; the page behind sections is a very light grey.
  • Green marks only primary actions, the active page, selected items, the verified tick and positive status.
  • Timber appears only as the brand mark's pith, the company monogram ring and sponsored labels.
  • Sections are one level deep: a white surface or an open rule, never cards inside cards.
  • The width you pick stays when you switch screens.

How it's built

  • tokens.css: primitive, semantic and component tokens, plus a data-density override.
  • components.css: one block per component, using semantic tokens only (checked: no hex values or primitives).
  • data.js: prototype content shaped like the existing contracts view models.
  • app.js: components as functions of props; pages only compose them. Comfortable and compact are one JobResultRow.

Screens

  • Find work at 1440 px with the details panel, and on a 390 px phone, where a job opens as a sheet.
  • A hauler business profile, as a landing operator sees it.
  • Equipment and availability management, with a "what landings see" preview.
  • A landing workspace overview that reuses JobResultRow in its poster context.
  • An inquiry and conversation workspace for one job.
  • The job editor (four steps with publish confirmations), hauler setup, and the add-a-landing form with public and private sections.
